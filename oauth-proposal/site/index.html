SIMULATION of a proposed flow. Anthropic doesn't offer "Sign in with Anthropic" for apps today. A local stand-in plays Anthropic's part.

Demo site

Ask Claude, paid from your own Anthropic API credits. You connect your account once, set a monthly limit, and never copy a key.

You'll choose a monthly spend limit and which models this site may use. The site gets a token that only works within those limits. You can revoke it at any time.

Manage this connection on the stand-in's Connected apps page.
